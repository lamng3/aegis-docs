    Commands

    CommandDoes
    aegis initCreate an object store (default .aegis)
    aegis put KIND FILEStore an ontology, KG, query, or profile; prints kind:id
    aegis get KIND:IDPrint a stored object
    aegis runs [--task T]List logged runs <Q, O, KG, R>
    aegis attack ONTO WORKLOAD [--budget k] [--search beam|uct] [--evaluations E] [--json F]Rank the classes the workload depends on and find the most harmful chains
    aegis detect ONTO WORKLOAD CHAIN [--threshold t]Apply a chain under the detector and report
